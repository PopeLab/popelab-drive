PopeLab Drive Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

PopeLab Drive is a personal rclone integration operated by Joshua Pope for access to the owner's Google Drive from owner-controlled computers.

Data accessed and purpose

The integration accesses authorized Google Drive file contents and metadata, including file names, folder structure, sizes, and modification times. It uses this information to display and manage files locally and perform requested transfers. Depending on the permissions granted, operations may include reading, creating, modifying, renaming, and deleting files and folders.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in local rclone configuration. Local devices may also retain file caches, downloaded files, and diagnostic logs containing file names or operational metadata. Owner-managed backups may retain copies of local configuration and data.

Local copies, logs, caches, and backups remain until removed through local cleanup or backup-retention procedures. Revoking Google access does not automatically delete previously downloaded files or local backups.

Use and sharing

The integration uses Google user data to provide its personal file-access and transfer functions. It does not sell that data, use it for advertising, or use it to train artificial-intelligence models.

Data is exchanged with Google and with owner-selected devices or destinations when the owner requests a transfer or backup. The integration does not publish Drive contents on this website.

The integration's use and transfer of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and revocation

The owner can stop the local integration and revoke its authorization through the Google Account's third-party connections settings. Local credentials and retained copies can be removed separately.

Website hosting

This information website adds no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process visitor information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the integration's data practices change.
